Watcher

Point the camera. Say what should happen. Get told when it does.

How it works
  1. Say what should happen. In plain words: the cat jumps onto the table, someone opens the door. Press + Add for each rule — up to five run at once, and you can add or remove them while watching.
  2. Press Watch. Every 1–10 seconds (your slider) a frame goes to a vision model that answers all your rules in one go. Quiet frames are skipped before they cost anything.
  3. Get the moment. When a rule flips — the cat appears, the door opens — it lands below with a snapshot. Scan the QR code and the same alert reaches your phone in Telegram, where you can also take a snapshot or edit the rules.

The counters show elapsed time, tokens used and what the session has cost. Nothing is stored: close the tab and the session is gone.
